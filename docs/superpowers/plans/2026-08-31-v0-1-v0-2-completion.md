# v0.1 v0.2 Completion Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Finish DaoDao AI Guild v0.1 and v0.2 scope while explicitly excluding deep room collaboration, blue-system deployment, opportunity pool, payment, settlement, and Oumi integration.

**Architecture:** Keep the current Next.js + FastAPI architecture. Treat v0.1 as community MVP polish and v0.2 as people-and-organization supply-side completion, with the backend as the source of truth for company and join-request state.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 14, React 18, TypeScript, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy async, Alembic, pytest.

**Spec:** Roadmap supplied by MAG on 2026-08-31; product structure captured in `D:\work\AgentStudyLLM\kb-wiki\wiki\concepts\daostore-guild-community.md`.

## Global Constraints

- v0.1 scope: internal pilot, Lobster Academy, question square, lobster rooms, Lobster Knight Profile.
- v0.2 scope: consulting company onboarding, Lobster Knight joins company, company management, platform review and governance admin.
- Room collaboration is not a current target; keep only room entry, room list/detail, basic membership/message capability already present.
- v0.3 blue-system deployment is out of this completion pass.
- v0.4-v0.8 enterprise opportunity pool, orders, payment, settlement, delivery, evaluation, and Oumi integration are out of this completion pass.
- Company Owner/Admin are company roles, not global user identities.
- Owner/Admin do not have to be Lobster Knights.
- A Lobster Knight can have at most one pending join request and at most one active company belonging.
- No invitation flow in v0.2; Lobster Knights find and apply to companies themselves.
- v0.2 does not support Owner transfer.
- v0.2 does not support Lobster Knights initiating release from company belonging; only company Owner/Admin can release.
